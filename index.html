The Bible & The Last Days

A scriptural examination comparing the NIV, KJV, and 1901 ASV

1. Why We Are Living in the Last Days

Scripture points to an invisible spiritual conflict as the underlying reason for worsening world distress[span_0](start_span)[span_0](end_span)[span_1](start_span)[span_1](end_span). Revelation reveals that Satan and his fallen angels were cast down out of heaven to the vicinity of the earth[span_2](start_span)[span_2](end_span)[span_3](start_span)[span_3](end_span). Because his remaining time is strictly limited before God establishes unhindered righteous rulership, anger and woe are intensified on the earth[span_4](start_span)[span_4](end_span)[span_5](start_span)[span_5](end_span).

Scripture Reference: Revelation 12:12
NIV "Therefore rejoice, you heavens and you who dwell in them! But woe to the earth and the sea, because the devil has gone down to you! He is filled with fury, because he knows that his time is short."
KJV "Therefore rejoice, ye heavens, and ye that dwell in them. Woe to the inhabiters of the earth and of the sea! for the devil is come down unto you, having great wrath, because he knoweth that he hath but a short time."
1901 ASV "Therefore rejoice, O heavens, and ye that dwell in them. Woe for the earth and for the sea: because the devil is gone down unto you, having great wrath, knowing that he hath but a short time."

2. How We Know We Are Living in the Last Days

The Bible does not provide a single isolated symptom, but a composite sign consisting of global physical crises, moral breakdown, and an unprecedented educational campaign[span_6](start_span)[span_6](end_span)[span_7](start_span)[span_7](end_span)[span_8](start_span)[span_8](end_span):

  • Physical Crises: Intensified warfare across borders, widespread food shortages, pestilences, and destructive earthquakes occurring successively[span_9](start_span)[span_9](end_span)[span_10](start_span)[span_10](end_span).
  • Moral & Social Breakdown: Severe decline in natural family affection, widespread self-centeredness, greed, brutal behavior, and hypocrisy[span_11](start_span)[span_11](end_span)[span_12](start_span)[span_12](end_span).
  • Global Kingdom Witness: The good news of God's Kingdom proclaimed to all nations as a testimony prior to the end[span_13](start_span)[span_13](end_span)[span_14](start_span)[span_14](end_span).

NIV "Nation will rise against nation, and kingdom against kingdom. There will be famines and earthquakes in various places."
KJV "For nation shall rise against nation, and kingdom against kingdom: and there shall be famines, and pestilences, and earthquakes, in divers places."
1901 ASV "For nation shall rise against nation, and kingdom against kingdom; and there shall be famines and earthquakes in divers places."

3. What the Last Days Entail

The "end of the world" in scripture denotes the culmination and termination of corrupt human systems and wickedness, rather than the literal destruction of planet Earth itself[span_15](start_span)[span_15](end_span)[span_16](start_span)[span_16](end_span). Biblical prophecy shows that the conclusion of this period leads into divine intervention, clearing wickedness away to make room for lasting peace and righteousness[span_17](start_span)[span_17](end_span)[span_18](start_span)[span_18](end_span)[span_19](start_span)[span_19](end_span):

  • Sudden Removal of Wickedness: The conclusion will catch an unmindful world unprepared, comparable to the days of Noah[span_20](start_span)[span_20](end_span)[span_21](start_span)[span_21](end_span).
  • Replacing Human Rulership: God's heavenly Kingdom will decisively replace divisive political powers[span_22](start_span)[span_22](end_span).
  • A Transformed Earth: The establishment of a "new earth"—a righteous human society freed from sickness, violence, and death[span_23](start_span)[span_23](end_span)[span_24](start_span)[span_24](end_span).
Scripture Reference: Daniel 2:44
NIV "In the time of those kings, the God of heaven will set up a kingdom that will never be destroyed, nor will it be left to another people. It will crush all those kingdoms and bring them to an end, but it will itself endure forever."
KJV "And in the days of these kings shall the God of heaven set up a kingdom, which shall never be destroyed: and the kingdom shall not be left to other people, but it shall break in pieces and consume all these kingdoms, and it shall stand for ever."
1901 ASV "And in the days of those kings shall the God of heaven set up a kingdom which shall never be destroyed, nor shall the sovereignty thereof be left to another people; but it shall break in pieces and consume all these kingdoms, and it shall stand for ever."
